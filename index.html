Your label size

Room Pricer Pro prints on 62mm x 29mm labels — Brother DK-1209 (small address labels, 800 per roll). Order these when you need more. The printer is a Brother QL-810W series.

Other roll sizes won't line up correctly, so stick with DK-1209.
